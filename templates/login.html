LabFlow

Every project, task and paper in your lab, in one place.

LabFlow is a research workspace for small lab teams: plan projects, run work on a Kanban board, track papers from idea to publication, and see who is overloaded before deadlines slip.

  • Drag-and-drop Kanban per project
  • Live dashboard: velocity, workload, deadlines
  • Publication pipeline from idea to DOI
  • Role-based access: Admin, Researcher, Intern
Django · Django REST Framework · MySQL · Vanilla JS
